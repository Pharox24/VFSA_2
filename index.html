ABOUT VFSA

Empowering Egyptian Workers Globally

VFSA is a specialized recruitment agency dedicated to connecting skilled Egyptian workers with promising overseas job opportunities. We're committed to empowering the local workforce through ethical, transparent recruitment practices.

From job placement and training to visa processing and post-arrival support, we handle every step of your journey abroad.
