Our Journey

The Story Behind VFSA

1995

Founded in Cairo by recruitment specialist Ahmed Khalil, VFSA started with a clear mission: to create ethical, transparent pathways for skilled Egyptian workers to access international employment. Our first office had just three employees and placed 50 workers in its first year.

2005

By 2005, we had expanded to five countries and placed over 1,000 workers. Our commitment to worker welfare and ethical practices became our hallmark, earning us the trust of families and employers alike.

Today

Today, VFSA is a leader in ethical recruitment, with 5,000+ successful placements across 15+ countries. We continue to innovate with free training programs and comprehensive post-placement support.
