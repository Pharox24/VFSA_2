THE VFSA PROCESS

From Application to Arrival

A streamlined, transparent process designed for efficiency and worker welfare

1

Application & Registration

Workers submit online application with personal details, work experience, and desired destinations. Takes only 10-15 minutes.

⏱️ Average time: 1-2 days

2

Screening & Interview

Our recruitment team reviews applications and conducts interviews to assess skills, experience, and language proficiency.

⏱️ Average time: 1-2 weeks

3

Training Program

Selected candidates receive free training in language, technical skills, and cultural orientation tailored to destination country.

⏱️ Duration: 2-8 weeks

4

Job Matching & Contract

We match workers with suitable employers. Contracts are reviewed and signed with full transparency on salary and conditions.

⏱️ Average time: 2-4 weeks

5

Visa Processing

Complete visa assistance including document preparation, attestation, embassy appointments, and work permit acquisition.

⏱️ Average time: 3-8 weeks (country dependent)

6

Pre-Departure & Arrival

Comprehensive orientation, flight arrangements, and arrival coordination. Ongoing support throughout contract.

✈️ Departure: Ready for travel
