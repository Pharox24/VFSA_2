Eligibility & User Representations

By registering with VFSA, you confirm that:

  • You are at least 21 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction).
  • All information you provide is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.
  • You possess valid travel documents (passport, visa) when required for placement.
  • You have no legal restrictions that would prevent you from working abroad.
  • Employers represent that they are legitimately registered entities and offer positions that comply with local labor laws.

VFSA reserves the right to verify credentials and reject any user who provides misleading information.

VFSA provides the following core services:

Candidate sourcing – database access & headhunting.

5

Worker Obligations & Conduct

As a candidate using VFSA, you agree to:

  • Provide authentic documents (CV, certificates, references).
  • Attend interviews promptly and communicate professionally.
  • Not accept multiple offers simultaneously without notifying VFSA.
  • Notify us immediately if you accept a position obtained outside VFSA that originated from our introduction.
  • Bear the cost of medical tests, passport, and any governmental fees (unless otherwise agreed with Employer).

Failure to comply may result in immediate termination of your profile.

6

Employer Obligations & Fair Hiring

Employers engaging VFSA must:

  • Provide detailed job descriptions, salary, benefits, and working conditions in writing.
  • Not charge any recruitment fees to candidates (zero-cost placement policy).
  • Comply with all labor laws of the destination country, including accommodation and insurance standards.
  • Inform VFSA of any changes to job offers or company status.
  • Respect candidate privacy and not share personal data without consent.

VFSA strictly prohibits exploitative practices. Violation leads to blacklisting and potential legal action.

7

Fees & Payment Terms

For Workers: Registration and basic job matching are free. A success fee (typically 10-15% of first month's salary) is charged only upon confirmed placement and signed contract, deducted from salary or paid by employer as agreed. VFSA never asks for money before a job offer.

For Employers: Recruitment fees are based on service level (contingency or retained). Invoices are due within 14 days. All fees are non-refundable after a candidate has accepted an offer.

💶 All amounts are in EUR or local currency equivalent, excluding VAT where applicable.

Placement & Post-Placement

Upon successful placement:

  • Worker and Employer sign a formal employment contract.

  • A replacement guarantee period of 90 days applies for certain roles (see contract).
  • Both parties agree to keep VFSA informed of any disputes or early terminations.

VFSA may request feedback and follow-up for quality assurance.
