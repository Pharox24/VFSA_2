About Us

Since the setting up of the company, we have promised to move realistically in preparing and providing workers according to the travel needs of the labor market, and then follow up on their procedures before signing and even signing agreements.

Our service even continued to track our workers through the first test of their participation in work, to ensure their suitability to the employers they have joined. This is done through a staff trained in all stages of selection, selection, and follow-up For Employment Services Abroad.
