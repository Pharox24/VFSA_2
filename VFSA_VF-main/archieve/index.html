About VFSA

Empowering Egyptian Talent Globally

A Short Introduction

VFSA is a specialized recruitment agency dedicated to connecting skilled Egyptian workers with promising overseas job opportunities. Committed to empowering the local workforce, VFSA provides comprehensive services, including:

Job Placement

Matching skills with international opportunities

Training Programs

Meeting international market standards

Visa Processing

Simplifying work permit acquisition

Personalized Support

Pre & post-placement assistance

From pre-departure orientation to post-placement assistance, we ensure a smooth transition and long-term success. By fostering growth and building a global community, VFSA strives to be a trusted partner for workers and employers, creating a seamless recruitment experience and enabling Egyptian talent to thrive internationally.
