WHAT YOU NEED

Requirements & Documents

✓ Basic Requirements

18+

Age Requirement

Must be 21-50 years old (varies by country)

Valid Passport

Minimum 2 years validity required

Work Experience

Minimum 2 years in your field (varies by position)

Educational Certificates

Attested copies of relevant qualifications

Document Checklist

  • Valid passport (minimum 2 years validity)
  • Recent passport-sized photos (6 copies)
  • Educational certificates (attested)
  • Work experience certificates
  • Professional licenses (if applicable)
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Medical examination report

Note: Requirements may vary by country and employer. Our team will guide you through specific requirements after application.
