Who We Are

Your Trusted Recruitment Partner

We combine on‑the‑ground knowledge with global connections to ensure safe, legal, and dignified work abroad.

VFSA is a growing company that seeks to fulfill societal needs for labor energy, which usually requires someone to extend a helping hand to it.

The company was established by a team with high experience in working outside the country, so you find them fully aware of the problems facing migrant workers and also meet the needs of the labor market for Egyptian workers.

Our Mission

To ethically connect Egyptian talent with global employers, ensuring mutual success and cultural fit.

Our Approach

How We Support You

From application to arrival and beyond, we're with you every step of the way.

1

Application & Screening

We receive employment applications, verify documents, and assess skills for those seeking work abroad.

2

Training & upskilling

Directing workers to partner training institutions to qualify for specific job requirements.

3

Contract & Visa processing

Follow up on contracts, coordinate embassy appointments, and ensure legal compliance.

4

3-Month Integration Follow-up

We check in after arrival to ensure smooth adaptation for both worker and employer.

Since our founding, we've promised to move realistically in preparing and providing workers according to market needs. We follow up on procedures before signing and even after agreements. Our service continues through the first test of work participation to ensure suitability for both workers and employers.
